Research

My research focuses on theoretical astrophysics and the physics of dense matter in neutron stars.

Neutron Stars and Dense Matter

I study the equation of state of dense matter inside neutron stars and investigate how the microscopic properties of matter affect neutron-star observables.

Hyperonic Matter

My work investigates the role of hyperons in dense neutron-star matter using relativistic mean-field models and different symmetry assumptions.

r-mode Instability

I study the stability of rotating neutron stars against r-mode oscillations and investigate the role of bulk viscosity in damping these instabilities.

Gravitational Waves

I investigate how the properties of dense matter and exotic particles in neutron stars can influence gravitational-wave signals from compact binary systems.
