About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, fascinated by the mysteries of the Universe and the extraordinary physics of its most extreme objects.

My research explores theoretical astrophysics, with a particular focus on neutron stars, dense matter, and exotic constituents such as hyperons and antikaon condensates. I am interested in understanding how these constituents influence neutron-star structure, stability, oscillations, and gravitational-wave signatures.

Research Interests

  • Neutron-star equation of state
  • Hyperonic matter
  • r-mode instability
  • Gravitational waves
  • Dense matter physics
